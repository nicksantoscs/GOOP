How Goop scores

Recovery blends last night's HRV vs your 7-day baseline (40%), resting HR vs baseline (25%), Garmin sleep score (25%) and Body Battery at wake (10%).

Strain is a 0–21 log scale from Garmin training load, intensity minutes and steps: 21 × (1 − e−load/250).

Health Monitor ranges are your mean ± 2 SD (with a minimum band) over up to 30 prior days.
